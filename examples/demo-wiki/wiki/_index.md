# Demo Wiki — Knowledge Management

A small, domain-neutral demonstration corpus about **the science of note-taking and personal knowledge management**. It exists so the literature-wiki template runs out-of-the-box without exposing any real research domain. Every page here is synthetic but illustrates the full set of conventions the real wiki uses: encyclopedic leads, subject-matter section headings, integrated `[[wikilink]]` citations, Obsidian callouts, and LaTeX math.
