## 引言

改革开放以来中国吸收外资经历了从无到有从少到多的过程近年来逆全球化思潮蔓延一此国家逐渐向贸易保护主义倾斜 国际经贸形势剧变 我国始终坚持改革开放的基本国策 不断扩大外商直接投资 的准入范围 谋求更高水平的开放 被认为是世界各国特别是发展中国家获取技术溢出 促进技术进步和长期经济增长的重要渠道 但是 也有学者对此提出质疑 认为在全球价值链背景下 世界各国按照自身的比较优势嵌入全球化的生产和分工<sup>［1］［2］</sup> 从研发创新的角度看 仅发达国家可以从全球价值链的垂直专业化分工中获益 而专门从事生产的国家则会遭受损失 面临着 低端锁定的风险<sup>［3］［4］</sup> 发展中国家通过吸收外资获取技术溢出的目的可能与外资利用东道国廉价生产要素的初衷相悖 的引进是否真正提升了发展中国家企业的创新能力仍然是一个有争议的问题

## （四） 是否越多越好？

上述的结论表明 总的来说 能够显著提高企业的创新能力 那么 是否应该完全放开外资准入限制 外商直接投资是否越多越好 上文对于 企业的判定方法是外商是否持有该上市公司的股份 按照这种界定方法 外资并不一定具备对上市公司的实际控制权 为了回答上述问题 我们进一步考察当外商对上市公司具有实际控制权 即上市公司的实际控制人或者控股股东为境外企业或者境外自然人时 对企业创新能力是否依然产生显著的提升作用 此外 当上市公司的董事长兼任总经理时 外商对上市公司的控制权和管理权趋于一致 可以在更大程度上执行外商的意志 因此 我们采用实际控制人是否为外商 控股股东是否为外商 实际控制人为外商且董事长与总经理合一 控股股东为外商且董事长与总经理合一 来衡量外商是否对企业

拥有控制权<sub>。</sub>

# The Innovation Effect of FDI Low<sub>－</sub>end Lockdown or Upgrading ——— Evidence from Shanghai and Shenzhen A－share Listed Enterprises

CHEN Bo， ZHANG Cheng－cheng， <sup>HUST</sup>

<sup>Abstract</sup>： How does FDI affect developing countries？ Low－end lockdown or upgrading？ Based on the data of Shanghai and Shenzhen A－share listed enterprises from 2007 to 2020， this paper studies the impact and channel of FDI on enterprise innovation． We find that FDI significantly improved the innovation of enterprises， and there exists spillover effect． The technology diffusion of FDI relies on the characteristics of FDI and absorptive capacity of host country. Distinguishing the quality of FDI, we find that the positive effect of FDI on firm enterprise is found more pronounced in the high－quality FDI from developed countries， the technology diffusion of FDI from Hong Kong, Macao and Taiwan is not obvious. Distinguishing the motivation of FDI, we find that the positive effect of FDI on firm innovation is more pronounced in horizontal FDI rather than vertical FDI. From the perspective of host country's absorptive capacity , intellectual property protection and financial development are positively moderating the FDI technology diffusion． Other than that， FDI technology diffusion of foreign-controlled enterprises is not obvious , thus more FDI is not always better.

<sup>Key words</sup>： FDI； enterprise innovation； FDI characteristics； absorptive capacity

# The Impact of Security Interest System Reform on Private Firms Leverage and Default Risk

Empirical Evidence Based on Natural Experiments in the Property Law

QIAN Xue－song， QIU Yu－cheng， <sup>HUST</sup>； TANG Ying－lun， <sup>Guangxi</sup> <sup>Minzu</sup> <sup>University</sup><sub>FANG</sub> <sub>Sheng，</sub> Beijing Normal University

<sup>Abstract</sup>： The problem of financing difficulties and high expense of finance has been plaguing the development of private firms in China， and the issues on how to improve the financing environment of private firms raises much concerns． we consider how the security interest reform induced by the property law enactment affects firm leverage and default risk in the perspective of legal environment. We find this reform significantly increased firm leverage. The triple-differences tests based on regional levels indicate that , compared with firms operating in provinces with more developed legal environment， firms operating in provinces with less developed legal environment experienced larger increase in the leverage． Further evidence shows that， instead of pushing up firm's default risk , the property law strengthens the ability of firms to repay debt to a certain extent when increasing firm leverage. This paper adds new evidence from China to the literatures of “law and finance" and helps to clarify the impact of the legal reform on firms’ leverage and default risk， which has important implications for improving the financing environment of private enterprises and accelerating the improvement of the socialist market economic system in the new era.

Key words: security interest system reform; property law; leverage; interest coverage
