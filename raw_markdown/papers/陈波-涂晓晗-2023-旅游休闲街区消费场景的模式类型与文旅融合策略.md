## <sub>（</sub>二<sub>）</sub>场景得分计算与描述性统计

计算场景各维度表现得分首先需要确定各项舒适物的权重 然后结合舒适物数量进行加权第一步 运用德尔菲法 邀请 位专家对 类舒适物进行独立打分 并对具有争议的舒适物进行二次协商确认 最终计算平均值得到消费舒适物在各子维度的权重 第二步 通过加权平均得到各旅游休闲街区在 个子维度上的表现得分 即各类消费舒适物子维度权重及其数量的乘积之和 除以该街区消费舒适物总量 计算公式如下

$$
\mathrm{S} _ {\mathrm{bd}} = \frac {\sum \mathrm{N} _ {\mathrm{x}} \mathrm{M} _ {\mathrm{x}}}{\sum \mathrm{N} _ {\mathrm{x}}}
$$

# Pattern Types and Cultural Tourism Integration Strategies of the Consumption Scenes of Tourism Leisure Street Blocks

Chen Bo ＆ Tu Xiaohan

<sup>Abstract</sup>： Tourism leisure street blocks are consumption scenes with unique cultural implications and aesthetic styles, which are of great significance to promote the cultural tourism integration and meet the needs of the people for a better life. This paper attempts to construct an interpretation framework of 3 main dimensions and 15 sub⁃dimensions of the consumption scenes of tourism leisure street blocks， and makes an empirical analysis by taking the first batch of 54 national tourism leisure street blocks as samples. The results show that the consumption scenes of tourism leisure street blocks can be divided into three pattern types: integrated development consumption scene, cultural experience consumption scene and commercial leisure consumption scene． Through the matrix combination of consumer amenities, the consumption scenes show different characteristics, whose features and value performance are comprehensively affected by local natural environment， economic level and cultural policies， showing distinct regionalism． When constructing various consumption scenes in tourism leisure street blocks， attention should be paid to the follvowing aspects： specifying the create theme with symbolic meaning, enhancing the audiences' cultural travel consumption experience in multiple dimensions， strengthening the protection and inheritance of local history and culture , and empowering the sustainable development of the city.
